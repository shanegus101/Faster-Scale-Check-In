Why check in?

      
Relapse is not an event — it’s a process. The FASTER scale names the slide that happens long before acting out, so you can get honest early, while the step back is still small. Read it top-down: Forgetting priorities → Angst → Speeding up → Ticked off → Exhausted → Relapse. Above it all sits Recovery: accepting life on God’s terms.

      
“Confess your sins to one another and pray for one another, that you may be healed.” — James 5:16

If you marked R — read this

      
A relapse is not the end of your recovery, and it is never the end of God’s love for you. Don’t wait for shame to pass before you tell someone — tell them now, within 24 hours. Shame says “hide.” Repentance says “come home.”

      
“If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.” — 1 John 1:9


      
Privacy & your data

      
All entries live in this browser’s local storage on this device. There is no account, no server, no analytics. Clearing your browser data — or using private/incognito mode — will erase your journal. Export a backup regularly.
