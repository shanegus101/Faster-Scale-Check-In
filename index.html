What is the FASTER Scale?

The FASTER Scale, developed by Michael Dye (The Genesis Process), describes the predictable slide from restoration toward relapse. Relapse rarely “just happens.” It usually starts days or weeks earlier with small shifts in priorities. The earlier you notice the slide, the easier it is to turn around.

Recovery: accepting life on God’s terms, with trust, vulnerability, and gratitude
F: Forgetting Priorities
A: Angst (Anxiety)
S: Speeding Up
T: Ticked Off
E: Exhausted
R: Relapse

How to use it

  1. Start with prayer. Ask God to show you the truth.
  2. Read the scale from the top down. Tap each stage and select every behavior you honestly notice. It’s normal to have some in several stages.
  3. Tap I’m here on the stage that best describes you. This is usually the lowest stage where you see several behaviors.
  4. Reflect using the three questions below, and name one next right step.
  5. Save it to your journal and share it with your accountability group.
  6. Look back over your journal to spot patterns and triggers.

The three questions

  • How does this behavior affect me? How do I act and feel?
  • How does this behavior impact the important people in my life?
  • How do I think I’m benefiting from this behavior? Is that healthy?

Sharing with your group

After saving, tap Share. On a phone, Share… opens your share sheet so you can pick Messages and choose your group chat. Text my group opens a new text with the report filled in. If you add your group’s phone numbers in Settings, it addresses them all. You can always choose whether to include your personal notes.

Privacy

Everything is stored only on your device using your browser’s local storage. There are no accounts, no servers, and no tracking.

If you’re in crisis

If you have thoughts of suicide or self-harm, call or text 988 (U.S.) or your local emergency number right away.

This tool supports, but doesn’t replace, your church community, recovery group, pastor, or a licensed counselor. Behaviors adapted from the FASTER Scale © Michael Dye, with additions specific to pornography recovery.

Add to your home screen

On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen. It will open like an app.
